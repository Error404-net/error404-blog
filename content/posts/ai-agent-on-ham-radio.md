I’m exploring what it would take to give an AI agent a voice on amateur radio through my station. The idea is to have it listen, call CQ, and hold natural conversations with nearby operators, with me monitoring and able to intervene. I’m looking at EchoLink, AllStarLink, and legacy SIP/VoIP radio gateways as connection options, with a custom MCP interface giving the agent tools to connect, check station status, and transmit speech.
